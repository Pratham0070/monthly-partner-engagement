03 · Do Earnings Drive the Sensex? — Yes. Always.

Source: Motilal Oswal · Trendlyne

When companies earn more, the market goes up. When they don't — markets eventually correct.

FY19–22

Healthy ✓

10–12%

EPS growth / year. Sensex rose steadily. P/E stable at 18–22x. Textbook healthy market.

FY22–24

Bull Run ↑

24%

EPS CAGR — best in a decade. Sensex: 47,000 → 85,000. Earnings led the way.

FY24–25

Risky !

2–6%

EPS growth very weak. Sensex stayed near 85,000 supported by SIP/DII flows, not fundamentals.

FY26+

Wait & Watch ?

?

Market has priced in earnings recovery. If delivered → rally continues. If not → correction likely.
