Geopolitical & Macro Drivers

India–US Trade Talks

Ongoing dialogue around tariffs and tech transfer; outcome remains pivotal for IT services and pharma exports.

Weak Monsoon Outlook (El Niño)

Below-normal monsoon probability flagged. Watch rural consumption, agri inputs, FMCG rural mix.

India–EU FTA

"Mother of all deals" — auto, dairy, IP at the center. Potential medium-term tailwind for export-led manufacturing.

Middle East / Oil Risk

Fragile ceasefire keeps crude event-driven. Every $10 move on Brent is a meaningful CAD / inflation lever for India.

FII Outflows & Rupee

FII selling pressure persists; DII and SIP flows offsetting. Rupee tracking dollar strength.

Defense Spending Surge

India–Pakistan stand-off aftermath driving defense capex. Listed defense PSUs in focus.

Outlook by Asset Class

Equity

DIIs and domestic retail SIPs continue to offset FII outflows. Large- and small-cap valuations normalizing. Selective opportunities in financials (sentiment-driven P/E compression) and industrials (capex tailwind). Earnings growth is the swing factor for the next leg.

Debt

Yield curve remains steep — short rates stable post RBI's neutral pause; long-end yields sticky on inflation and fiscal worries. Expect curve to remain steep at the ends. Globally elevated yields keep volatility alive.

Commodities

Crude: Brent ~$96/bbl — up ~5% from late-May as the Strait of Hormuz supply-risk premium crept back despite intermittent US–Iran diplomatic progress; still event-driven, CAD / inflation lever active. Gold: ₹1.53 L per 10g — eased from ₹1.56 L as risk appetite firmed; hedge bid intact, central-bank demand (China-led) underpins medium-term upside. Silver: ₹2.65 L per kg — softer on the week; weak global-growth drag offset by industrial demand.
